About VASTRA MART

VASTRA MART is a customer-friendly e-commerce project based in Bhojuveer, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. The platform is designed to bring everyday shopping categories together in one easy-to-use website.

Customers can search products, browse categories, add items to the cart, choose a payment mode, generate an invoice, view orders and submit feedback. The project also includes an owner panel for stock management.
